Frequently asked questions

How do I install the tracking snippet?

Copy the snippet from Settings → Project and paste it before the closing head tag.

Can I import historical data?

Yes, via CSV or our API (Team plan and above).

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in the US by default. EU data residency is available on Business and Enterprise plans.

Do you support Google sign-in?

Yes, on all plans. SAML SSO is available on Business and Enterprise.
